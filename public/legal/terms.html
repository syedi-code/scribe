Scribe answers questions about philosophy and theory from a library of books, and checks that every quotation it gives is on the page it cites. It is made and run by one person, Ibrahim Syed, and these terms are the agreement between you and Ibrahim Syed when you use it.

Plans

  • Free: 20 questions a month, answered by GPT-5.6 Luna. No card needed.
  • Paid: $20 a month. 150 questions a month, answered by Claude Sonnet 5 unless you choose another model, and the scan of the printed page behind each quotation.

Questions reset on the 1st of each month, at midnight UTC. Unused questions do not carry over. Limits are stated as numbers, and those numbers may go up. If a limit on a plan you pay for is ever lowered, you will be told in Scribe at least 30 days before it takes effect.

Paying, cancelling and refunds

  • Paid is billed monthly by Stripe, from the day you subscribe, until you cancel. Prices are in US dollars. Tax is added where it applies.
  • Cancel whenever you like, from Account → Manage billing. Paid stays on until the end of the month you have paid for, then your account goes back to Free. Your conversations stay yours to read.
  • Refunds: ask within 30 days of a payment and it will be refunded. No questions. Write to support@socialeating.studio.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries again for a few days and Paid stays on meanwhile. If it keeps failing, the subscription ends and your account goes back to Free.

What Scribe can and cannot promise

  • Scribe runs on AI models, and it can be wrong: about context, about emphasis, about what an author meant. When a quotation is marked found on the page, the words are in the book exactly as quoted. That does not mean the answer reads them well. Open the page when it matters.
  • Some books were scanned with imperfect text, so a genuine quotation can occasionally fail its check.
  • Scribe is offered as it is. It may be slow, it may be down, and its features may change. It is not a substitute for reading the books.

Using it fairly

  • Scribe is for asking questions and reading the passages it cites. Do not use it to copy the books themselves, and do not try to get around the limits, the checks, or the page-by-page access to scans.
  • Do not use it to break the law or to harm anyone.
  • An account is for one person. Keep your sign-in to yourself.

An account used against these terms may be closed. If you pay and that happens, the unused part of the month is refunded.

Your questions

What you type stays yours. You allow Scribe to send it to the AI company answering, to keep it for 30 days, and to read it to improve Scribe, as the privacy page describes.
