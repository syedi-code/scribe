scribe-lm answers questions about philosophy and theory from a library of books, and checks every quotation it gives against the page it cites. It is made and run by Social Eating Studio. These terms are the agreement between you and Social Eating Studio when you use it. If something here seems unfair or unclear, write to support@socialeating.studio and it will be answered by the person who wrote it.

The plans

  • Free: 10 questions a month. No card, no trial to forget about.
  • Paid — $20 a month: 150 questions a month, upgraded models (Claude Sonnet 5 today, and better ones as they arrive), and the scan of the printed page behind each quotation.

Questions reset on the 1st of each month, at midnight UTC. Unused questions do not carry over. The limits are stated as numbers, and those numbers can go up. If a limit on a plan you pay for is ever lowered, you will be told inside Scribe at least 30 days before it takes effect.

Paying, cancelling and refunds

  • Paid is billed monthly by Stripe, from the day you subscribe, until you cancel. Prices are in US dollars, and tax is added where it applies.
  • Cancel whenever you like, from Account → Manage billing. Paid stays on until the end of the month you have already paid for, and then the account goes back to Free. Your conversations stay yours to read.
  • Ask within 30 days of a payment and it will be refunded, no questions. Write to support@socialeating.studio.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries again over a few days and Paid stays on meanwhile. If it keeps failing, the subscription ends and the account goes back to Free.

What to expect from the answers

Scribe is a reading tool. It searches its library, reads the pages it finds, and quotes them — and then checks each quotation, word for word, against the page it cites.

  • A quotation marked found on the page really is in the book, as quoted. That is a fact about the words, not about the argument: an answer can quote accurately and still read a passage poorly. Open the page when it matters — that is what the link is for.
  • Scribe runs on AI models, so it can be wrong about context, emphasis or what an author meant.
  • Some older books were scanned imperfectly, so a genuine quotation occasionally fails its check. Scribe says so rather than hiding it.
  • It is one person's project. It may be slow, it may be down for a while, and features will change. It is offered as it is, and it is no substitute for reading the books.

Using it fairly

Scribe is for asking questions and reading the passages behind the answers. Two things to keep to: do not use it to reproduce the books themselves, and do not work around the limits or the page-by-page access to scans. Beyond that, use it as much as your plan allows, for work, study or curiosity.

An account is for one person. If an account is used against these terms it may be closed, and if it was paid, the rest of the month is refunded.

Your questions

What you type stays yours. By using Scribe you allow it to be sent to the AI company answering, kept for 30 days, and read to improve Scribe, as the privacy page sets out in full.
