Privacy

Last changed 22 September 2026

Please don't put anything private in your questions. What you type is sent to an AI company to be answered, is kept for 30 days, and may be read to make Scribe better. Ask it about books, not about yourself.

scribe-lm is run by Social Eating Studio. This page is the whole of what it keeps and who else sees it. It is short because the practice is simple, and it is meant to be read rather than filed.

What is kept

  • Your account: the email address you signed in with, and your name if the service you used shares it. Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access, through GitHub or Google.
  • Your conversations: your questions, the answers, and which pages of which books were read to write them.
  • A count of your questions: one line per question, with the model used and how large the request was. This is what the monthly limit is counted from, and what says how much a question cost to run. It holds none of your words.
  • If you pay: Stripe's reference for you and for your subscription — whether it is active, and when it renews or ends. Card details go to Stripe and never reach Scribe.

Scribe sets one cookie, to keep you signed in, and remembers in your own browser which notices it has already shown you. There are no advertising trackers and no analytics, and your IP address is not stored.

Who else sees it

  • The AI company that answers. Your question, the conversation so far, and the pages Scribe reads to answer are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on the model you choose.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts Scribe and its database, and handles sign-in.
  • Stripe, if you pay, which takes the payment.

Nothing is sold, and nothing goes to anyone else.

How conversations are used

They are read to improve Scribe: the instructions it gives the model, the way it searches and reads, and the way it checks quotations. That is the reason they are kept at all, and the reason they are kept for 30 days rather than for ever.

How long it is kept

  • A conversation is deleted 30 days after its last message, along with its answers and the citations in them. Not archived, not anonymised: deleted, by a job that runs every day.
  • A conversation you delete yourself goes within a day.
  • The count of your questions is kept while your account exists, because it is the record of what was used and paid for.
  • Payment records are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires of them.

Deleting your account

Write to support@socialeating.studio from the address you signed in with, and the account and everything tied to it will be deleted. If you pay, cancel first from your account, or say so in the email and it will be done for you.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that affects you, Scribe will say so before the change takes effect, and the date at the top will change.

Questions about any of this: support@socialeating.studio
