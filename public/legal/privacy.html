Privacy

Last changed 22 September 2026

Do not put anything private in your questions. What you type is sent to an AI company to be answered, is kept for 30 days, and may be read to make Scribe better. Ask about books, not about yourself.

What Scribe keeps

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if the service you signed in with shares it. Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access, through GitHub or Google.
  • Your conversations: your questions, the answers, and which pages of which books were read to write them.
  • A count of your questions: one record per question, with the model used and its size. This is how the monthly limit is counted and what each question costs to run.
  • If you pay: Stripe's reference for you and your subscription (whether it is active, and when it renews or ends). Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Scribe.

Scribe uses one cookie, to keep you signed in, and remembers in your browser which notices it has already shown you. There are no advertising trackers and no analytics. Scribe does not store your IP address.

Who else sees it

  • The AI company that answers. Your question, the conversation so far and the pages Scribe reads are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on the model chosen, to write the answer.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts Scribe and its database and handles sign-in.
  • Stripe, if you pay, which processes the payment.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

How your conversations are used

Conversations are read to improve Scribe: its instructions to the model, the way it searches and reads, and the way it checks its quotations. That is the reason they are kept at all, and the reason for the 30 days below.

How long it is kept

  • A conversation is deleted 30 days after its last message, with its answers and everything attached to them. Not archived, not anonymised: deleted.
  • A conversation you delete yourself is removed within a day.
  • The count of your questions is kept while your account exists, because it is the record of what was used and paid for. It holds no words from your questions.
  • Payment records are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account

Write to support@socialeating.studio from the address you signed in with, and your account and everything tied to it will be deleted. If you pay, cancel first from your account, or say so in the email and it will be done for you.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, it will say so in Scribe before the change takes effect. The date at the top is when it last changed.

Questions about any of this: support@socialeating.studio
